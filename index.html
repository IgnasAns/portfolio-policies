Every app listed here is designed to work entirely offline. Each one stores all of its data locally on your device, has no account system and no sign-in, contains no analytics and no advertising SDK, and does not transmit your personal data to the developer or to anyone else. The only network use is Google Play's own billing service for optional in-app purchases, which Google processes under Google's privacy policy. You can delete all app data at any time by uninstalling the app or by using its in-app delete or reset controls.
